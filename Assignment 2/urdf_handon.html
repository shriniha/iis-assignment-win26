URDF Design Challenge

The Domestic Assistant Robot

Apply modularity and parameterization to design a functional robot for the drink serving task.

The Challenge: Serving Eva a Drink

Your task is to design a simple robot world system model in URDF (using Xacro for modularity) capable of reaching into a kitchen fridge to fetch a milk carton and pouring it into Eva's mug on the counter.

You must use composition (separate files for base, arm, and gripper) and parameterization (using ${param_name} variables) to make your robot easy to adjust for different counter heights or reach requirements.

Environment Specifications

Refrigerator (Milk Target)

  • Fridge Door Shelves: Located between 0.4 m and 1.2 m height.
  • Target Item: Milk Carton (0.1 m W x 0.1 m D x 0.2 m H).
  • Task Requirement: Arm must lower down to 0.4 m to grasp the carton cleanly.

Kitchen Counter (Mug Target)

  • Counter Height: Flat surface at 0.9 m height.
  • Target Item: Eva's Mug (0.08 m radius, 0.12 m height).
  • Task Requirement: Arm must lift to 1.0 m height to pour milk into the mug without colliding with the counter.

These dimensions dictate the vertical reach of your manipulator links and the required gripper opening width.

Key Robot Components

1. Base Component (base.xacro)

  • Define a mobile base or simple cylindrical pedestal.
  • Use parameters for size: ${base_radius}, ${base_height}.

2. Manipulator Arm (arm.xacro)

  • Design a simple 2-DOF or 3-DOF arm (revolute or prismatic joints).
  • Ensure total reach covers 0.4 m to 1.0 m height range.
  • Parameterize link lengths: ${link1_length}, ${link2_length}.

3. End-Effector (gripper.xacro)

  • Create a simple 2-finger parallel gripper.
  • Parameterize max finger opening span: ${gripper_span} (must open wide enough to fit the 0.1 m carton).

4. Main Robot File (robot.xacro)

  • Include all sub-files using <xacro:include> tags.
  • Instantiate components and attach them via joints to build the full robot assembly.

Submission Checklist & Defense

Be prepared to verify your URDF in Rviz or Binder by demonstrating:

  • Parameter Flexibility: Changing ${link1_length} successfully updates the entire arm without broken joint transforms.
  • Reach Range: The end-effector frame can reach both the fridge shelf (0.4 m) and counter height (1.0 m).
  • Modular Structure: All four .xacro files are cleanly separated and imported correctly.
